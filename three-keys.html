Three Keys for a First Build

The Build Room · Session 1 Scoping Frame
1
Focus on one thing.
Not two things. Not a platform. One thing.
2
One thing with impact for you.
Creates business, gains money, or saves you time. Real impact. Not a demo that sits on a shelf.
3
Focus on one part of that thing.
A small, central piece. Not the whole system. The door into the system.
One thing. One thing with impact. One part of that thing.
Alex Salinsky / The Build Room
