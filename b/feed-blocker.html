Cut the feeds out of Reddit, Instagram, YouTube and Facebook

A small Chrome extension that keeps messages and search but blocks the feeds, or makes you breathe and chase a button to get to them.

Install it (about a minute)

  1. Unzip the download somewhere you will keep it.
  2. In Chrome, Edge or Brave, go to chrome://extensions.
  3. Turn on Developer mode (top right).
  4. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.

After that it runs on its own. Download it here.

What it does

One Chrome extension, a different level of friction per site. Reddit's home page and the popular feeds go to a grumpy cartoon bouncer who reminds you that you decided Reddit is bad for you, with a breathing circle and a nudge to drink water; subreddits and posts still open. Instagram's home feed, Explore and Reels open blank: you take three guided breaths, then catch a Show the feed button that runs from your mouse five times. Messages, notifications and profiles open normally. YouTube's home page gets the same gate, Shorts always get the bouncer, and videos play with no up-next column, no end-screen suggestions and autoplay off. Facebook's feed, stories, reels and Watch always land on Messages; Marketplace, groups and profiles still work.

Why

The feeds are where the negativity and the lost hours come from, but the useful parts of these sites (messages, a specific subreddit, a search) live next door. Blocking whole sites throws those away, so this only cuts the parts built to hijack attention, and makes the rest a deliberate choice.

How it works

  1. You open a page or click inside a site reddit.com, Reels, Shorts, Facebook home
  2. Page rules check the address Chrome declarativeNetRequest
  3. In-site watchers content scripts
  4. Block, gate or redirect bouncer page, breathe-and-chase, Messages

What you connect

  • Chrome with Developer mode on, to load the unpacked extension (Chrome, Edge, Brave)
