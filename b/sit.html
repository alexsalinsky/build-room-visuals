Offline meditation timer with singing bowl bells and your own guided tracks

A meditation app that installs from a web page onto an Android home screen and runs with no internet: swipe a time, tap begin, and a soft bowl bell starts and ends the sit.

Install it (about a minute)

  1. Open the link on your Android phone in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots, then Add to home screen, then Install.
  3. Open it from the new icon and tap add to load your own guided audio files.
  4. Turn your media volume up: the app cannot change it for you.

After that it runs on its own. Download it here.

What it does

A meditation app that installs from a web page onto an Android home screen and runs with no internet. You swipe a band left or right to pick the minutes, and it opens at the last time you used. Tap begin, get a few seconds to settle, then a soft singing bowl bell rings; three bells close the sit, with an optional bell every 5 to 30 minutes to refocus. Below the timer sit your own guided meditations, copied onto the phone from its files and played inside the app. The bell is synthesized in the browser, and the whole sit is rendered as one audio track so it keeps going with the screen locked, the way music does.

Why

The meditation timer app Alex relied on stopped starting. He wanted the same simple ritual back, plus a few guided tracks one tap away, with nothing that needs a connection or an account.

How it works

  1. Pick a time swipe band, remembers last
  2. Render the sit bell plus silence, one audio file
  3. Play with screen locked browser audio player
  4. Guided tracks files saved on the phone

What you connect

  • A static web host for the one-time install (Cloudflare Pages, GitHub Pages, Netlify)
  • Your own audio files on the phone, mp3 or m4a
