Hey Audrey,

You're at Adopting (10/20). Honest baseline. You're using AI on real work but not yet building with it.

What that means

You're past the "I tried it once" stage. You use AI when it fits and you trust your read on its output. You haven't woven it into how your role actually runs, and you haven't built something other people use yet. That's the gap, and it's a normal one for someone with your role.

Your goal: bump to Operating

Operating people make AI part of how their role gets done. The shift is from "I use AI when I think of it" to "AI is in my daily workflow."

3 things to do this month

  1. Wire it in. Pick one weekly task you do every Monday and make AI the first step. (Build)
  2. Connect it. Hook your AI to one tool you already live in: your inbox, your calendar, or your task list. (Connect)
  3. Tell one teammate. Walk them through what you built and why it worked. Five minutes, no slides. (Spread)

Why a cohort accelerates this

The plan above is doable solo. But people who actually move levels do it in a group.

  • Accountability. Peers waiting on you Monday morning is the difference between "I'll get to it" and shipping.
  • Patterns travel. Someone else's breakthrough becomes yours in real time. You skip the dead ends they hit.
  • Deadlines beat permission. A weekly demo, even an ugly one, forces the work.
