How to run the room

Every cohort, every session. Three rules, read this 10 minutes before you start.

The three rules

1
Ledger time is for the next step, not troubleshooting
The point of ledger review is to get each builder to their next step, fast. The moment an exchange turns into debugging, stop it. Note it, move on.
2
Troubleshooting happens in the build hour
1:1 or small group, at your discretion. That's where you sit with someone and untangle the thing. Not in front of the whole room.
3
Prompt builders to articulate demo, prototype, WHY and HOW
Keep pulling them back to the story: what they'll demo, what the prototype is, and how this build helps them personally or in their business. Builders who can say it out loud build faster.

What good ledger time sounds like

60-90 secThat's the budget per builder. Total, not per question.
Where"Where are you right now?" One sentence back.
Next step"What's your next step?" If they can't name it, help them name it. That's the whole job.
Lock itSay it back to them so the room hears it. "So this session you're getting the form to submit."
Move onNext builder. Don't linger, don't solve.
If someone's stuck: "That sounds like a build-hour problem. I'll come find you." Then actually come find them.

Coaching questions that surface the story

Demo
"What will you show on Demo Day? Walk me through the 60 seconds."
Prototype
"What does the thing actually do today, if I sat down at your screen right now?"
Why
"What changes for you, or for your business, once this works?"
How
"Who uses it, and when? Monday morning, what does it save you?"
If a builder can answer all four crisply, they're on track. If one answer is mushy, that's their next step.

During the build hour

  • Rotate 1:1s at your discretion. Start with whoever you flagged during ledger time. Small groups are fine when two builders share the same wall.
  • Park deep rabbit holes. If a fix is going past a few minutes, help them park it and pick a next step they can move on today.
  • Protect the room's momentum. The build hour belongs to everyone. One builder's blocker shouldn't become the room's soundtrack.
