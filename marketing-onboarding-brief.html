01What The Build Room is

The three rules (the most important thing in this document):
Learn AI. Build Robots. Stay Human.
That line is the brand. Verbatim, never paraphrased, in every asset that uses it.

The Build Room takes regular people (operators, founders, and their teams) and has them build something real with AI in four weeks. Cohort-based, outcome-oriented: everyone ships by Demo Day. It's a founder-led brand built around Alex Salinsky's facilitation. Don't position it as "AI training" or "AI fluency"; the product is the transformation, the confidence of having built something yourself.

The purpose (from Alex's Horizons of Focus, 2026-07-12): watch people transform. The win is their confidence, crossing from hopeless to empowered and still human. AI is the vehicle, not the point.

Non-negotiable principles:

  • Technology serves us, not drives us.
  • You will change. You will make a product. This is not a course.
  • Nobody rejects themselves on my watch.
  • No step-by-step framework. You tackle your problem, with coaching.
  • No fabricated proof. Real wins, real names (with permission), real numbers.
  • The emotional element is permanent.

Format: 4 weeks, one live ~2-hour session per week, builds between sessions, Demo Day at the end. A "Session Zero" kickoff precedes S1-S4.

03Goals, the honest hierarchy

  • Vision (2029-2031): a company with coaches and systems, empowering entire business teams. Alex earns ~$20K/mo. Permanent wait list; opportunities come to us.
  • By July 2028: Alex's Build Room income $10-20K/mo. A thriving community on a monthly rhythm. Likely one full-time coach.
  • The hardest date: first business-team client by March 2027. Alex's own words: "Without business clients, I don't know if we have a thing here."
  • Explicitly NOT a goal: retreats. They're 2026 revenue plays, not the long-term engine.
What this means for marketing: the corporate / closed-cohort motion matters more than any single launch. Kickoff Sessions sell open cohorts and retreats; they probably don't sell closed cohorts. That path runs through conferences (LPI Learning Live, London, Sep 16-17) and direct outreach through friends and affiliates.

06The obvious gaps

1 · No marketing owner

Alex's own Horizons note: "I don't know how to do it. Needs an owner or a system." This is the seat being recruited for.

2 · No attribution

Nobody knows which channel produces buyers. No UTMs enforced, no "where did you hear about us" at checkout.

3 · Proof is trapped

155 of 178 proof entries are raw. The best material the business owns sits unpublished for lack of permission asks and clip cutting.

4 · Publish-step stall

3 newsletter editions in 5 months, no talks or podcasts shipped, finished reels unposted. Assets get built, then stall at publish.

5 · No corporate pipeline

First business-team client by Mar 2027 is the existential goal. Today the pipeline is two warm names and one conference.

6 · CAC unknown

No paid channel ever tested. One $200 LinkedIn experiment planned for the PDC retreat.

7 · Thin top of funnel

A 30-day engagement scrape found ~16 LinkedIn reactors and ~12 IG engagers. The launch engine assumes audiences that don't exist yet.

8 · Founder bottleneck

Alex travels Sep-Nov exactly when all three launches peak. Whatever is designed must run without him some weeks.

9 · Alumni layer undefined. Membership format and price are parked until Feb 2027, so post-cohort energy has nowhere to go except Skool.

08Brand and voice rules (hard, don't re-litigate)

  • Thesis line verbatim wherever used: "Learn AI. Build Robots. Stay Human."
  • The live event is a "Kickoff Session." Never "webinar" or "masterclass."
  • No em dashes in any copy. Ever.
  • Stripe link in chat once per Kickoff Session. No pressure closes.
  • No fabricated proof. Only Proof Library entries cleared for public go public. Real names only with permission. Never publish putdown quotes, even cleared ones.
  • No invented URLs or domains (buildroom.work does not exist).
  • No black-box visual treatment in decks. No "drop it in the chat" audience prompts.
  • Cohort ads: no. Retreat ads: small test only; kill if CAC exceeds 2x tier-1 margin.
  • Skool stays engagement-gated, not open-by-affiliate-link.
  • Voice: direct, peer-to-peer, contractions on, no corporate filler. Samples: the LinkedIn newsletter + jess-pack/01-tone-of-voice.md.
  • Sliding-scale pricing and per-builder performance details stay private. Client-facing artifacts show role + name only.
