5 · Descriptive Post (LinkedIn, organic)

Alex's voice, first person, story-led. Also works trimmed for Skool.

AS
Alex Salinsky
The Build Room · Learn AI. Build Robots. Stay Human.
I built an AI coach that argues with you about scope. It's called the Drafting Table. You bring the idea you've been circling for months, and in about ten minutes it pins down three things: the dream behind it, the smallest version worth building, and how you'd demo it to someone. Then it does the part people actually thank me for. It writes your starter prompt. Not a template. A prompt in your words, about your build, that ends by making the AI summarize what it thinks you want and ask clarifying questions before it writes a single line. That last step is the whole trick. Most people paste a vague idea into ChatGPT, get a vague result, and decide AI isn't for them. The Drafting Table makes the machine prove it understood you first. We've run this inside our cohorts all year. Kate, the self-described least technical person on her team, shipped a live tool her CEO said they'd spent two months trying to hire a developer for. It starts with a plan. The open version is free. Ten minutes, no signup to start, and you leave with a plan saved to your own link. Comment "TABLE" or DM me and I'll send it to you.
Comment-gate ("TABLE") keeps the link out of the post so LinkedIn doesn't suppress reach, and every comment is a warm DM opening.
