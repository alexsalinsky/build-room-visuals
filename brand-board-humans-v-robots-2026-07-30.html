The V rule

The V appears exactly once per page in its wordmark form. Beyond that, it recurs as a section divider (angled rule), as a wedge in the hero, and as the pointer on CTAs. It is a structural motif, not a decoration.

Three don'ts
  • Don't ever tilt this playful. The V is confident, not cute. No sound-effect typography, no exclamation points.
  • Don't use clay and graphite on the same block of copy. They meet at the V, not on a paragraph.
  • Don't stack Fraunces italic in a paragraph. It's a moment font. One line at a time.
